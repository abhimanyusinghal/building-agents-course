# INC-2381 — Login throttle retry storm

## What happened
A mobile release ignored Retry-After and retried throttled logins in a tight
loop. ERR_AUTH_1042 volume rose 40x and the auth pods saturated. The throttle
worked; the clients did not honour it.

## Lesson
Treat a 429 without client back-off as a sev-2, not as "working as designed".
